How it works

Three simple steps

No sign-ups and no waiting for uploads to finish.

Send a file

Send or forward any video, audio, image, or document to the bot on Telegram.

Get your link

The bot replies with a direct stream link and a download link for your file.

Watch or download

Open the web player to watch right away, or share the link with anyone.

Features

Built to be fast and light

Everything runs on a single small server.

Instant streaming

Playback starts right away and you can seek anywhere in the file, since the server supports range requests.

{{if .Player}}

YouTube-style player

Custom controls, keyboard shortcuts, playback speed, theater mode, picture-in-picture, and subtitles.

{{end}}

Multi-worker

Traffic is spread across several bot workers, so big files stay fast.

Expiring links

Links can expire after a set time, and download counts are tracked for each one.

Secure links

Every link carries its own hash, so only people with the link can open the file.

Low resource use

Written in Go and made to run on low-power servers. Files are streamed through, not converted.

Passwords & one-time links

Protect a link with a password, limit how often it can be opened, or choose its expiry per link.

Inside Telegram

Open your files as a Mini App, share links with inline mode, and add Watch buttons to your channel posts.

My Files & collections

Search, rename, organise in folders, and share whole collections on one page. Available in 6 languages.

API & webhooks

Manage links with API keys and get signed webhook events. Read the docs.
